Dr. Mikah's love for chiropractic began when she was a little girl recovering from a dance injury. What started as a way to get back to doing what she loved soon became a regular part of her life, and she grew up experiencing the benefits of chiropractic care firsthand.

In high school, Dr. Mikah went to work for the same chiropractor she'd grown up seeing. This time, she got to experience chiropractic from the other side of the adjusting table. She saw firsthand the relationships he built with his patients and how much they trusted him. She realized that being a chiropractor wasn't just about helping people feel better- it was about getting to know them, caring about their lives, and becoming someone they could count on.

And that's exactly the kind of chiropractor she wanted to become.

Having always had a heart for women and children, Dr. Mikah found that chiropractic gave her the perfect opportunity to combine the things she loved most: caring for people, building relationships, and being part of a family's story.

Today, nothing makes her happier than caring for a first-time mama. She loves being part of the excitement, the nerves, and all the big feelings that come with pregnancy. Whether that means helping a mom feel more comfortable, offering reassurance, or sharing a few happy tears, she's all in. And getting to meet that sweet baby after months of caring for Mom? That's the icing on the cake.

She's just as happy surrounded by energetic little kids who are running, climbing, exploring, and keeping everyone on their toes. Dr. Mikah loves meeting kids exactly where they are and making their visits feel fun, comfortable, and completely their own.

If you ask Dr. Mikah what matters most to her, she'll tell you it's ensuring every patient knows they have someone in their corner.

She's a listener, a feeler, and a self-proclaimed overexplainer. She wants to know what's going on in your life, not just what's going on with your body. She'll take the time to explain the why behind your care, make sure you feel comfortable, and remember that random story you told her three visits ago. Her goal is for every patient to leave feeling a little more confident, a lot more cared for, and genuinely excited to come back.

It's exactly why Vitality felt like home from the moment she walked through the doors. She fell in love with the warm, family-centered atmosphere and couldn't wait to become part of the growing South College Station community.

Outside the office? Dr. Mikah is happiest with a good cup of coffee, a great movie, and absolutely nowhere she has to be early the next morning.

She's a dedicated thrifter, adventurous foodie, homemade latte enthusiast, and proud aunt who loves spending time with her nephew. She loves experimenting with new recipes, discovering little coffee shops, and exploring new places through their food.

Her perfect Saturday involves sleeping in, making coffee, watching a Wes Anderson movie, getting in a workout, wandering through an antique shop, cooking something delicious, and catching a movie with a friend- but please make sure she's in bed by 9 p.m.

Dr. Mikah hopes that when you visit Vitality, you'll find someone who genuinely wants to know your story, celebrates the little milestones, and makes you feel like you've got a friend in your corner.

And if your kids start asking when they get to see Dr. Mikah again? Even better.
