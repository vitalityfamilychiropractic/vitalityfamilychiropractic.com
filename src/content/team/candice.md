Candice's journey into massage therapy began when she was just 14 years old and struggling with chronic migraines. After her mom introduced her to lymphatic and craniosacral therapy, she experienced firsthand just how powerful gentle, intentional bodywork could be. What started with a healthy dose of skepticism turned into a lifelong passion for helping others feel their best.

Born right here in Bryan, Candice grew up between Texas and Utah before eventually finding her way back to Aggieland. Today, she and her husband are raising their blended family of seven kids (yes, SEVEN!), which means life is full of sports, busy schedules, and plenty of opportunities to practice patience! She loves cooking for her family, studying God's Word, hot yoga, and spontaneous dance parties—even if she's the only one dancing.

What Candice loves most about massage therapy is walking alongside her clients as they heal. Whether she's helping someone find relief from physical discomfort or simply creating space for their body and mind to relax, she considers it a blessing to witness the difference that care can make in someone's life. And getting to know the people on her table? That's one of her favorite parts.
