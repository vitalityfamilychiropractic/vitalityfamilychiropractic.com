Dr. Christie's love for chiropractic started long before she ever imagined opening her own practice.

As a teenager, she struggled with migraines that traditional medicine hadn't been able to resolve. When she began seeing a chiropractor, something clicked. She loved the experience so much that she started looking for excuses to get adjusted weekly. What began as a search for answers eventually became a calling.

Today, Dr. Christie is the founder of Vitality Family Chiropractic, where she gets to do what she loves most: care for families, build lasting relationships, and remind people just how incredible their bodies are.

With advanced training in prenatal and pediatric chiropractic, infant care, and chiropractic cranial work, Dr. Christie has a particular love for the very beginning of life. She holds her Certification by the Academy Council of Chiropractic Pediatrics (CACCP), is certified in the Webster Technique, holds an advanced perinatal certification through the ICPA, and has pursued extensive continuing education in perinatal, pediatric, and cranial care.

And if you ask her what she's best at? It's probably babies.

Some of her patients have even accused her of being a bit of a baby whisperer. Whether she's gently working with a newborn, helping a mom catch a break, or winning over a tiny patient who isn't quite sure about this whole chiropractor thing, caring for babies is where she feels most at home.

But ask Dr. Christie what really makes her job special, and she probably won't start listing certifications.

She'll tell you about the families she's cared for through multiple pregnancies. The babies she's watched grow into big kids. The families who started as patients and became friends. She'll remember your family's last vacation, what your kids are excited about, and that little detail you mentioned three appointments ago. Because to Dr. Christie, those aren't little things. They're the things that make you you.

For her, chiropractic has never been just about an adjustment. It's about being invited into people's lives and getting to love on them along the way.

Dr. Christie believes Vitality is a gift from God, and she doesn't take that responsibility lightly. Her faith shapes the way she leads, serves, and loves the families entrusted to her care. She's always dreamed of creating more than a place to get adjusted — a space where families feel known, kids can be kids, and appointments turn into conversations, friendships, and hopefully the best 15 minutes of someone's week.

Outside the office? Well, sitting still isn't exactly her specialty.

Dr. Christie is married to Nick and is mom to Mackenzie, her favorite adventure buddy. She's an eternal optimist, always dreaming up her next big idea and finding joy in the everyday moments. Her friends and family have mostly given up trying to get her to slow down.

Her hope is that when you walk into the office, you'll find more than a chiropractor. You'll find someone who believes in your body, cheers for your family, celebrates your milestones, and genuinely looks forward to seeing you.

Because around here, you're not just a patient. You're part of the family.
