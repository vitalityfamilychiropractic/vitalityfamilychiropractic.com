Yvonne is a Bryan-College Station girl through and through! Born and raised right here in Aggieland, she briefly traded her hometown for Hutto when her husband’s job took the family away. But even while living in Hutto, Yvonne continued making the drive back to College Station to work at the same office. If there’s one thing to know about Yvonne, it’s that when she loves her people, she sticks with them!

And Yvonne has a LOT of people to love! She and her husband have three boys she absolutely adores, along with a wonderfully enormous extended family, most of whom live right here in the area. She’s the unofficial mom and nurse of just about everyone she knows, the person people call when they’re sick, hurt, or just need someone to take care of them. She credits that nurturing spirit to her own mom, and it’s easy to see how naturally it carries over into her role at Vitality. She can even hold a newborn while keeping the entire front office running, which might just be her greatest superpower!

Outside the office, Yvonne loves football, fishing trips to Rockport with her boys, and wandering through farmers markets and local festivals. Her perfect Saturday starts with a little extra sleep, coffee and devotions on the porch, and ends with football and family. Her faith is at the heart of her life, and she loves being part of a practice where caring for people and building genuine relationships matter so much.
