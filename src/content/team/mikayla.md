Mikayla may have been born in Florida, but she'll be the first to tell you she's a Texas girl! She grew up in the tiny town of Dime Box before making her way to College Station to attend Texas A&amp;M. She's graduating with her degree in Animal Science in December 2026, but Aggieland isn't getting rid of her just yet! She'll be sticking around for graduate school, too.

If there's one thing to know about Mikayla, it's that she has a huge heart for people and a knack for staying busy! Along with serving on the executive board of Sigma Alpha sorority, she's a mentor through AggieACHIEVEMates, helping young adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities experience college life. She also serves as a special needs intern at Central Baptist Church. That same love for people is one of her favorite parts of Vitality, where she especially loves following our expecting mamas through pregnancy, meeting their sweet new babies, and watching our little patients grow!

Outside the office, Mikayla is one of seven siblings, a proud dog mom to her Bernedoodle, Adagio, and the designated planner of her friend group (backup plans included!). She loves needlepoint, baking, Aggie football, and a good Target run when she needs absolutely nothing. Whether she's catching up with friends over coffee or spending a cozy Saturday night at home, she's happiest surrounded by the people she loves.
