Muscles and joints do not work in isolation. When a joint has been out of
position for a long time, the surrounding soft tissue adapts around it — and
after an adjustment, that same tissue can pull the joint straight back where it
was. Massage therapy addresses the muscular half of that picture, so
adjustments hold longer and feel more comfortable.

## How we use it

Massage is offered as a standalone appointment or scheduled alongside a
chiropractic visit. Sessions are tailored to what your body is asking for
rather than a fixed routine, and your therapist works directly with
{{lead}} so both halves of your care are pulling in the same direction.

## Who it helps

- **Anyone under active chiropractic care** whose adjustments are not holding
  as long as they would like
- **Pregnancy and postpartum**, where the load on the low back, hips and
  shoulders changes week to week
- **Desk workers and athletes** carrying tension through the neck, shoulders
  and mid-back
- **Anyone who simply wants an hour to themselves.** Not everything has to be a
  treatment plan
